# DJ-s-Cyber-Portfolio
## 👋 About Me  
I first became interested in cybersecurity after a recommendation from a close friend I served with, who later became a SANS ACS and graduate student. His guidance opened the door to a field that combines technical problem-solving with constant learning — something that immediately fit my personality and strengths. I’ve always enjoyed understanding how systems work, how they can be protected, and how attackers think, and I’m drawn to the fact that cybersecurity requires continuous improvement and adaptation. The idea that the field never stops evolving is what keeps me motivated to grow my skills every day.

## 🎯 My CTF Experience  
Participating in cybersecurity competitions like the National Cyber League was a major turning point for me. I enjoyed the challenge of being placed under pressure and having to learn new skills quickly to solve problems in real time. Working alongside teammates and sharing strategies helped me understand how defenders collaborate in real environments, and I found that I genuinely enjoyed the investigative mindset required to break down unfamiliar scenarios.

## ⭐ Highlights From My CTF Participation  
While respecting NCL’s policies (no challenge write-ups or restricted details), here are some personal performance highlights and experiences:

- **Thrived under pressure** — learned how to reduce complex problems into manageable steps even with a timer counting down.   
- **Developed strong teamwork habits** — collaborating with new teammates, dividing tasks efficiently, and combining skill sets to gain advantages.  
- **Improved real-world defensive skills** — including log analysis, traffic interpretation, credential security concepts, and vulnerability identification.   
- **Strengthened my ability to self-learn quickly** — every competition introduced new tools, methodologies, and techniques I needed to pick up on the fly. I enjoyed the exposure and the wide range of topics covered. 

These experiences reinforced my interest in cyber defense, problem-solving, friendly competition and continuous improvement.
